## Japan Interview Tips

1. **Honesty wins** - "I don't know, but I would approach it like this..."
2. **Show your thinking** - Talk through your thought process
3. **Be humble** - Show eagerness to learn
4. **Know YOUR code** - Understand your project deeply
5. **Prepare self-intro** - Practice a clear 2-minute introduction

---

## Daily Format (1 hour)

```
First 30 min:  Watch/Read (passive learning)
Next 20 min:   Take notes or trace code (light active)
Last 10 min:   Explain concept out loud (retention)
```
